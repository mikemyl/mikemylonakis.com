Hi, I'm Mike, a software engineer based in Athens, Greece.

I am a Senior Software Engineer at [TransFICC](https://transficc.com/), a London fintech that builds
low-latency e-trading connectivity and workflows for global banks. Before that, I was a Director in the
eFX quant development team at UBS in London, and I led the DevOps team at Copper.

On the side, I work as a Solutions Architect with [Parsectix](https://parsectix.com/), where I help
companies move to the cloud and run well on it: from secure AWS foundations to data platforms and
delivery pipelines.

I write here about Linux, Go, infrastructure and the things I learn on the way. Opinions are my own and
not those of my employer.
